EEPROM UPDATE

You are going to update the EEPROM (factory data) partition on the device.
It holds the MAC addresses and the WiFi calibration data, so be sure to use a dump taken from a device of the very same model.
Please, choose file from your local hard drive and click Upload button.

Size: xxxx
MD5: xxxx

If all information above is correct, click "Update".

WARNINGS
  • do not power off the device during update
  • if everything goes well, the device will restart
  • updating the EEPROM with a dump from another model may break WiFi and wired network
  • keep a backup before updating, use the "Backup" button below
